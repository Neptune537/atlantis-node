# CYAN economy proposal for Atlantis

Status: design for review. No CYAN mint, conversion rate, contribution program, or public sale is authorized by this document.

## What Atlantis already has

| Area | Evidence in this checkout | What it means for CYAN |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ledger | [Tide Ledger](crates/tide_ledger/src/lib.rs) has double-entry transfers, SQLite persistence, Coral escrow and state channels. Balances are keyed by account UUID and stored as `f64`; the asset tag is on the journal entry. The generic transfer function lacks a finite-positive amount check, and account seeding writes directly to the balance map. | Reuse the accounting design. Add an asset-aware integer journal before putting multiple assets or real value through it. |
| Settlement and governance | [`solana_programs`](crates/solana_programs/src/lib.rs) models CYAN supply, quadratic stake voting, three pillar tallies, and timelocked multisig disbursements. Its [manifest](crates/solana_programs/Cargo.toml) has no Anchor or Solana program dependency. | Treat this as Rust reference logic. This checkout does not establish a deployed contract, mainnet mint, circulating supply, or custody arrangement. |
| Store Node funding | The [Store Node handler](crates/home_node/src/main.rs) calls an annual escrow flow that takes an existing ledger balance, locks at least $12, and releases the first $1. A separate $10 qualification rule exists in the subscription/governance models. | This legacy flow does not implement annual membership dues or the store revenue share. It must not create an extra Store Node charge or be used as a pre-launch billing path. |
| Identity and access | [Club protocol](crates/atlantis_protocol/src/social_club.rs) defines club roles and membership tiers. | Issue entitlements to verified members by club and tier. Clients consume the same entitlement; the voice service validates room access independently. |
| Clients | [HeavenlyBound](../../heavenlyBound/dist/index.html) queries SOL for a fixed public address. [Dashboard functions](../../dashboard/functions/src) already use Firestore for tasks, bids and escrow-related features. | A fixed SOL lookup is not a player wallet. Audit the existing Firestore money paths before integrating them. Neither client should infer a balance or credit from a market feed, a placeholder, or another wallet. |

## Two tiers

**Gameplay.** Aether Shards are earned through play and spent on mana thresholds, gem sockets, and crafting. Tithe Credits are earned through game objectives and spent on deterministic in-game services or crafting recipes. Both are integer units, non-transferable between players at launch, and have no cash-out or conversion to CYAN. An Innie Ascent d20 encounter may grant ordinary gameplay rewards. It cannot pay out CYAN, a transferable collectible, or a chance at something with real-world value. Outie Sanctum recipes may produce ordinary in-game cards and runes.

Tide Ledger is the target source of truth. Partition balances by account and asset; make each journal entry atomic with its balance changes and unique by operation ID. A trusted match authority supplies the completion receipt and the server computes the reward. Two clients, retries, and offline replay must credit it at most once. Cloud Run can host a service or projector; Firestore can publish read models; Sheets can report on reconciled totals. None should write directly to the gameplay journal from a client. One authority commits each journal. A disconnected Club Node queues signed receipts or runs a separately scoped local game, and neither Ocean nor another Tier 2 device is required to be online. Show cached balances as stale and queued rewards as pending until committed.

**Platform.** CYAN is the platform asset name and the identifier already used by the settlement model and radio-tip conventions. Verify whether a real CYAN mint, liabilities, integrations, or balances exist before minting or changing any settlement integration. Treat the modeled cap as a code parameter until the mint and supply policy are verified.

Make club access work with identity and membership without a wallet. A future optional token proof can grant a defined perk through the same entitlement service. It should not grant base voice access by default or multiply competitive stats, gameplay loot, cash rewards, or votes in other pillars. Fused cards and body runes can be useful as in-game cosmetics now. If tokenized versions are ever introduced, give them explicit ownership and deterministic acquisition rules; don't promise CYAN redemption or price backing. Pick the minimum Token-2022 extensions and document mint, freeze, upgrade, and custody authorities before a devnet pilot; test wallet and DEX compatibility with that exact configuration. See [Solana's token extensions reference](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions).

## Wallet tied to Google sign-in

Use the same wallet profile in HeavenlyBound and Dashboard, including Android and Quest. The identity service verifies Google's ID token signature, issuer, audience and expiry, and checks the nonce where applicable. It maps the verified Google issuer and stable `sub` to a private Atlantis principal. Never key the wallet by email or the whole ID token. Follow [Google's OpenID Connect verification guidance](https://developers.google.com/identity/openid-connect/openid-connect).

In the first release, Google sign-in opens the player's wallet profile. Linking an existing Solana wallet requires a signed, expiring one-time challenge bound to that authenticated session and the wallet address. Persist the verified address against the Atlantis principal. Query native SOL and the configured, verified CYAN mint separately; if no wallet is linked, show that explicitly. Require wallet signatures for on-chain spending. Keep email and Google subject identifiers off-chain. Require fresh authentication and proof of control for linking or replacing a wallet; recovery of a Google account does not recover the wallet's private key. An automatically created wallet would need a separate signing, custody and recovery design. Never derive private keys from Google credentials.

The current [Home Node verifier](crates/home_node/src/lib.rs) checks token shape and signature length, hashes the whole token into the identity, and stores the encoded payload as `google_sub`. It must be replaced before relying on it to bind a wallet. Verify that different sign-in tokens for the same Google account resolve to the same Atlantis principal, and that forged or replayed credentials and cross-account wallet-link attempts fail. Until this exists, the fixed SOL address must not be represented as the player's wallet.

## Treasury and community funding

- Start the real operating reserve at zero, with no insider allocation or seed balances presented as funded assets. The current Home Node seeds a modeled 100,000 CYAN subscription reserve. Reconcile or isolate that reserve before reporting any real balances; zero treasury and zero token supply are separate claims.
- **Annual membership:** Starting at official launch, every user pays $12 USD per year, including store owners. The fee is once per member per year, even when the member owns multiple stores. It is a membership due, with no separate $1 monthly charge and no CYAN stake required.
- **Store revenue share:** Starting at official launch, each store pays the platform 10% of eligible store income, separate from the owner's annual membership. Before enabling settlement, define eligible income explicitly: which sales count and how tax, shipping, discounts, refunds, chargebacks, and processor fees affect the base. Reconcile the platform share against completed transactions and their adjustments.
- **Pre-launch is free:** Disable both fee paths server-side until official launch is explicitly activated. Do not collect dues or store shares, start renewals, or accrue charges for later collection during the preview. Pre-launch activity is not billed retroactively. Track membership dues and store revenue shares separately when billing goes live.
- Separate restricted deposits, operating funds, and any future liquidity inventory. Give the operating fund budgeted spending with multisig approval, a timelock, and auditable receipts. A permanently locked principal cannot pay for infrastructure. Keep real funds out of the current Store Node routes until authentication and ownership checks, verified payment receipts, idempotency, and restart reconciliation are in place.
- Keep any ROSCA separate from the operating treasury. A rotating savings pool needs scheduled participant payouts, defaults, exits and disputes; it cannot double as permanent infrastructure funding. Start physical bounties with a separately funded, capped Coral escrow and require a signed venue or organizer attestation plus evidence of completion. Location proofs can help establish presence. A radio/network proof alone cannot show someone completed a physical task. Any real-money ROSCA needs a separate review of custody, payments, and applicable rules.

## Governance

Keep Core Builders, Active Stakers and Community Delegates as three equally weighted chambers. Give each chamber a proposal path and a fixed share of the tally. For the first version, snapshot verified principals and chamber eligibility per proposal. One principal gets one chamber per proposal; stake voting may use bounded quadratic weight on verifiable stake in that chamber only. Require published quorum and passage rules and separate disbursement authorization. Use one independently authorized ballot per chamber, enforce the voting deadline, and test the failure and recovery paths.

The current model cannot enforce this design as written: its vote path requires qualifying stake and accepts builder and community weights as arguments on the same ballot; finalization also needs an explicit check against early closure. A member without stake must be able to vote in an eligible non-stake chamber. A pilot can validate the charter with nonbinding votes before giving it spending authority.

## Supporter funding through CYAN

- **Primary CYAN sale.** The operating company would sell an identified allocation of CYAN and receive the proceeds for development and infrastructure. Disclose the amount available, price, allocation and release schedule, buyer rights, intended use of funds, and transfer restrictions. A purchase from another holder on a DEX generally sends value to the seller or liquidity pool. Treat that separately from the company's fundraising.
- **Review the offering before building checkout.** If the company sells CYAN to finance development and buyers reasonably expect profits from the team's work, the offering may involve an investment contract under U.S. law even if CYAN has platform uses. Have securities counsel determine the offering structure, eligible buyers, required disclosures, and resale rules. If Regulation Crowdfunding fits the issuer and terms, confirm that a registered intermediary can support the proposed offering and delivery. See the [SEC crypto asset interpretation](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2026/03/s7-2026-09) and [Regulation Crowdfunding rules](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/exempt-offerings/regulation-crowdfunding).
- **Keep the funding flows distinct.** The Google-linked wallet can receive CYAN after a verified purchase settles under the offering terms. The $12 annual membership and 10% store share fund operations separately and begin at official launch. Keep token purchase commitments and payments closed during the free preview under the current launch rule. The sale needs its own readiness decision even after the platform launches.

### Other funding routes

- **Invest in the company.** If the operating company is an eligible U.S. issuer, evaluate a Regulation Crowdfunding offering through a registered broker-dealer or funding portal. Decide with counsel what security to offer and what rights it carries. Prepare the company records, ownership table, financial statements, funding target, budget, and Form C disclosures. The intermediary handles the investment transaction and its payment flow. Keep investment proceeds in the issuer's accounts with a separate ledger from membership dues, store fees, and CYAN. See the [SEC issuer guide](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/regulation-crowdfunding-guidance-issuers).
- **Support the project.** A separate voluntary contribution can fund a named milestone such as Quest testing or the first Club Node rollout, with no ownership or financial return. Confirm the receiving entity and accounting before opening payments. Describe this as project support and reserve charitable deduction claims for contributions to a qualified organization. See the [IRS guidance on charitable contributions](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc506).
- **Prepare during the free preview.** Draft a private offering outline and budget first. If the team chooses to publicly measure investor interest before a Regulation Crowdfunding filing, have counsel and the intermediary review the materials under Rule 206 and collect interest only, with no money or commitments.

## Rollout

1. **First playable release:** Award and spend one off-chain currency in a real game loop, then show the same authenticated balance and receipt history in HeavenlyBound and Dashboard. Test duplicate rewards, cross-asset spending, reconnects, and crash recovery before adding the second currency. Keep SOL and token views optional and bind any wallet view to the connected player. No financial action is needed to play.
2. **Free club pilot:** Use signed club entitlements for private rooms and server-authorized voice grants; run nonbinding votes. Publish planned spending categories such as relays, radio, and moderation. Keep all platform billing disabled through the pilot.
3. **Settlement pilot:** Audit amount validation, direct balance seeding, per-asset accounting, authority checks and financial route exposure. Verify payment and on-chain reconciliation, then test the proposed CYAN mint configuration and treasury on devnet. Confirm Google Play and Quest distribution rules before adding any purchase or asset feature. [Google Play's blockchain policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13607354?hl=en) restricts taking anything of monetary value for a chance at an NFT of unknown value outside eligible programs.
4. **CYAN offering, if pursued.** Confirm the issuer, offering terms, settlement process, and buyer eligibility before opening a primary sale. Assess any later secondary market against the offering's transfer restrictions and the verified mint configuration.

Official launch billing does not depend on a public CYAN market. Before switching on billing, verify that preview accounts and pre-launch sales cannot generate charges, one store owner with multiple stores receives one annual membership charge, and retries cannot duplicate either charge.
